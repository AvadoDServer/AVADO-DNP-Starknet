Fast start: downloading a recent copy of Starknet

Instead of replaying Starknet from its first block (which takes weeks), the box downloads a recent copy that the Juno team publishes, then catches up the last few days by itself. The copy is unpacked while it downloads.

The fast start download stopped

The box tries again by itself every 30 minutes.

Install an Ethereum node first

Your Starknet node checks its work against Ethereum, so it needs an Ethereum node on this AVADO. Install Geth or Nethermind from the store. If you stake ETH on this box, you already have one.

Your Starknet node starts by itself once it finds one. Nothing downloads before that.

Become a Starknet validator

Your box can earn STRK rewards by confirming, about once an hour, that it sees the latest Starknet blocks (an “attestation”).

You need at least 20,000 STRK of your own. It stays in your own wallet: the box only keeps the key of a small, separate account that signs the attestations.

Before you stake: how it works
  • You need at least 20,000 STRK of your own to stake. It stays under the control of your own wallet. This box never sees that wallet or its key.
  • The box signs attestations with a separate, small account that only pays the tiny fees (Starknet calls it the “operational account”). That is the only key the box keeps.
  • Rewards go to a reward address in your own wallet. If the box misses an attestation, you get no reward for that hour.
  • Getting your stake back takes 7 days after you ask for it.

Not enough STRK? About delegation

The 20,000 STRK must be your own. StarkWare's delegation programme adds millions of STRK to a few validators, but it is meant for professional operators: you must already run a validator, show 99% uptime for three months on Starknet and its test network, pass a compliance check and apply in a short window. It promises nothing. Once your validator runs, other STRK holders can also delegate their STRK to you if you open your validator for delegation (a "pool") on the staking page of your wallet. See StarkWare's delegation programme.

Set it up in four steps

  1. Wait until the Starknet node is up to date. The status at the top of this page turns green.
  2. Make a new account for attestations in your Starknet wallet (Ready, formerly Argent, or Braavos). Send it about 100 STRK for fees and make one transaction with it so the wallet deploys it. Do not switch on extra protection for this account (a Guardian, two-factor or a hardware signer): the box must be able to sign on its own.
  3. Stake from your main account: at least 20,000 STRK, with the new account as the operational address and an address of yours for rewards. Follow the Starknet validator guide (steps “approve” and “stake”).
  4. Enter the new account below: its address and its private key (in your wallet's settings, “Export private key”).

Only enter the key of the small attestation account, never the key or recovery phrase of the account that holds your stake. The key is stored on this box only, is never shown again, and is used for nothing but attestations.
